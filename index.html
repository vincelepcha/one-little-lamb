A Counting Lullaby

1

One little lamb has gone to bed,

2

Two little clouds above your head.

3

Three shining stars have found their place,

4

Four soft kisses on your face.

5

Five slow breaths and in you go,

6

Six sweet dreams all in a row.

78

Seven, eight, the night is deep,

910

Nine, then ten, and off to sleep.

1

Sleep, my lamb, the counting’s done. You were always number one.

Dedicated to my daughter, whom I sing to sleep with this lullaby. We have counted lambs, clouds and stars together, every night. She will always be my number one.
